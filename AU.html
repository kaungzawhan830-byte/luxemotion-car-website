ABOUT LUXE MOTION

Driving excellence since 2005 with a passion for premium vehicles and exceptional customer service.

OUR HISTORY

From humble beginnings to becoming a leader in the premium automotive industry

2005 - Foundation

LuxeMotion was founded by automotive enthusiasts with a vision to redefine luxury car buying experience.

2008 - First Expansion

Opened our second dealership location and expanded our inventory to include European luxury brands.

2012 - Online Platform

Launched our comprehensive online platform, allowing customers to browse and reserve vehicles digitally.

2016 - National Recognition

Received the "Premium Dealer of the Year" award for outstanding customer service and sales excellence.

2020 - Digital Transformation

Implemented VR showrooms and AI-powered vehicle matching to enhance the customer experience.

2023 - Present Day

Now operating 12 locations nationwide with over 50,000 satisfied customers and counting.
