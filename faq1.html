Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our vehicles and services

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What is your vehicle inspection process?

All vehicles at LuxeMotion undergo a rigorous 150-point inspection process. This includes mechanical inspection, safety checks, cosmetic evaluation, and performance testing. Our certified technicians ensure every vehicle meets our high standards before being offered for sale.

Do you offer test drives?

Yes, we encourage all potential buyers to schedule a test drive. You can book a test drive online or by calling our dealership. We recommend scheduling in advance to ensure the vehicle you're interested in is available.

Can I reserve a vehicle?

Yes, you can reserve a vehicle with a refundable deposit of $500. This will hold the vehicle for you for up to 7 days while you complete your financing arrangements or make other preparations.

What financing options do you offer?

We work with multiple financial institutions to offer competitive financing options, including traditional auto loans, lease options, and special financing programs for qualified buyers. Our finance specialists will help you find the best option for your budget and needs.

What credit score is needed for approval?

We work with buyers across the credit spectrum. While higher credit scores typically qualify for better rates, we have programs available for various credit situations. We encourage you to apply regardless of your credit history - our team will work to find you the best possible solution.

Do you offer pre-approval?

Yes, we offer online pre-approval that typically takes just minutes. This helps you understand your budget before you start shopping and makes the purchasing process faster once you find the right vehicle.

Do you provide maintenance services?

Yes, our state-of-the-art service center provides comprehensive maintenance and repair services for all vehicles, regardless of where they were purchased. Our certified technicians use genuine parts and the latest diagnostic equipment.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day/500-mile return policy on most vehicles. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return the vehicle within this period for a full refund, subject to certain conditions. Some specialty or high-mileage vehicles may have different return policies.

Do you offer vehicle delivery?

Yes, we offer vehicle delivery within a 100-mile radius of our dealership at no additional cost. For longer distances, we can arrange shipping at competitive rates through our trusted partners.

What warranty comes with your vehicles?

All our vehicles come with a comprehensive 3-month/3,000-mile warranty that covers major components. Additionally, we offer extended warranty options that can provide coverage for up to 5 years or 100,000 miles.

Are certified pre-owned vehicles available?

Yes, we offer a selection of certified pre-owned vehicles that undergo additional inspections and come with extended warranty coverage. These vehicles typically have lower mileage and are in excellent condition.

What if I have issues after purchase?

Our customer support team is available to assist with any issues after your purchase. Simply contact our service department to schedule an appointment. Most warranty repairs are completed within 24-48 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including cash, bank transfers, credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), and financing through our partner banks. We also work with all major Myanmar banks for financing options.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer flexible installment plans through our partner banks and financial institutions. You can choose from various tenure options ranging from 12 to 60 months, depending on the vehicle and your eligibility.

Do you accept trade-ins?

Yes, we accept trade-ins and offer competitive valuations for your current vehicle. The value of your trade-in can be applied directly toward your new vehicle purchase, reducing your financing needs.
