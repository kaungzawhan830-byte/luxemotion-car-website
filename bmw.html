Bayerische Motoren Werke AG

BMW is a German multinational manufacturer of luxury vehicles and motorcycles headquartered in Munich, Bavaria, Germany. The company was founded in 1916 as a manufacturer of aircraft engines, which it produced from 1917 until 1918 and again from 1933 to 1945.

BMW is one of the best-selling luxury automobile brands in the world. The company is a component of the Euro Stoxx 50 stock market index. BMW established its main production facilities in Germany, Brazil, China, India, South Africa, the United Kingdom, and the United States.
